GAMEPLAY ROLES

Runner
- Moves on elevated obstacle course
- Respawns on fall

Sabotager
- Moves on ground
- Uses abilities to disrupt runners

Supported player counts
- Up to 4 Runners
- Up to 4 Sabotagers



ABILITY SYSTEM

Sabotager Ability

Mechanics
- SphereCast from camera
- Target layer: Runner

Scripts

KNOCKBACK SYSTEM

Receives and processes knockback forces.

Scripts
- KnockbackReceiver

Integration

RESPAWN SYSTEM

Detects player falling off course and respawns them.

Scripts
- RespawnOnFall

Mechanics
If player Y < killY
→ teleport to spawn point

CharacterController disabled during teleport.
